## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, laboratory-grade biomechanical retail environment. It is tailored for technical runners, track athletes, and footwear enthusiasts who evaluate gear through empirical metrics and athletic innovation. The emotional response is centered on focus, kinetic energy, and precision engineering.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision High-Tech** with **Performance Athletics**:
- Deep asphalt and carbon surfaces serve as the baseline testing environment.
- Laser-sharp cyan accents `#06B6D4` illuminate critical biometric data, tactile touchpoints, and real-time state changes.
- Layouts are dense yet structured, displaying technical shoe specifications (stack height, heel-to-toe drop, energy return percentages) alongside striking product silhouettes.
- Micro-interactions are snappy, direct, and tactile, simulating precision-calibrated machinery.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an ultra-dark track environment with high-contrast scientific readouts:

- **Primary (`#06B6D4`):** Technical Cyan. Represents propulsion, kinetic energy, active selections, and interactive touch states.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Bio-Green. Applied to optimal physiological metrics, verified gait indicators, sustainability certifications, and neutral pronation tags.
- **Tertiary (`#F97316`):** Hyper-Orange. Reserved exclusively for urgency, speed alerts, race-day drops, high energy-return indices, and checkout triggers.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Deep Asphalt. Acts as the deep charcoal bedrock across all views, supported by tiered surface levels (`#1E293B` for container surfaces, `#334155` for high-contrast borders).
- **Text & Foreground:** High-legibility crisp off-white (`#F8FAFC`) for primary headers and data values, with muted cool gray (`#94A3B8`) for secondary labels and metric units.

## Typography

Typography establishes an engineered, technical reading rhythm. Headings feature **Manrope**, combining clean modernist geometry with tight numerical figures. Body text and analytical data points use **Hanken Grotesk** for optical balance and legibility under active motion.

- **Numerics & Readouts:** Tabular lining figures are enforced across product specification cards to align measurements, weights, and drops precisely.
- **Labels & Tags:** Rendered in uppercase with subtle tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em` to `0.08em`) to reinforce laboratory taxonomy.
- **Mobile Scalability:** Display and headline levels drop by 20–25% on mobile viewports to prevent awkward line breaks on long footwear model names while preserving punchy visual impact.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 4-column mobile grid that scales to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop. Mobile interaction zones are prioritized for single-thumb ergonomics:

- **Touch Reachability:** Action trays, size selectors, and filter triggers sit in the lower 40% of the viewport.
- **Data Densities:** Product grids run on a tight `gutter-sm` (0.75rem / 12px) on mobile screens to present shoe specs side-by-side without overflowing horizontal bounds.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Component internals adhere to standard multiples of 4px, where micro metric tags use `space-xs` and container paddings use `space-md`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft diffuse drop shadows. Instead, it relies on **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Subtle Luminescent Borders** and **Backdrop Transmission**:

1. **Ground Base (`#0B1120`):** The primary app canvas background.
2. **Elevated Surface (`#0F172A`):** Product cards, carousel tiles, and metric blocks with a 1px border (`#1E293B`).
3. **Floating Surface (`#1E293B`):** Bottom sheets, modal dialogs, and sticky floating cart bars. Enhanced with a subtle top edge highlight of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)` to simulate overhead lab lighting.
4. **Active/Glow Tier:** Focused input fields and selected size chips emit a 0 0 12px `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)` direct emission glow rather than an ambient shadow.
5. **Translucent Overlays:** Fixed navigation headers use `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` with a 12px backdrop blur filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) for layering clarity during scrolling.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled, technical corner geometry (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Micro Components (Chips, Tags, Metric Badges):** 0.25rem (4px) corner radii, giving a structured, card-punched appearance.
- **Standard Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** 0.25rem (4px) up to 0.5rem (8px) (`rounded-lg`), balancing ergonomic contact with precision lines.
- **Cards & Sheets:** 0.5rem (8px) on standard cards, 0.75rem (12px) (`rounded-xl`) on sheet top corners for smooth slide-over surfaces.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to floating quick-filter pills and active cart badge indicators to ensure visual contrast against angular footwear packaging.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Technical Cyan (`#06B6D4`) background with deep obsidian text (`#0F172A`), uppercase `label-lg`, height `48px` minimum for mobile tap safety. Border radius `4px`. Hover/Active: brightness 1.1 with cyan border flare.
- **Secondary CTA:** Asphalt surface (`#1E293B`) with 1px border (`#334155`), white text (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Critical Action (Buy Now / Race Drop):** Solid Hyper-Orange (`#F97316`) fill with white text and active kinetic feedback.

### Metric Chips & Pronation Tags
- **Biometric Badges:** Stacked micro-containers displaying running stats (e.g., "DROP: 8mm", "WEIGHT: 210g", "GAIT: Neutral").
- Background: `#0B1120`, border: 1px solid `#1E293B`, label: `label-sm` in `#94A3B8`, value: `label-md` in `#06B6D4` or `#10B981`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Vertical layout featuring a 1:1 square shoe imagery canvas with an ambient dark gradient backdrop (`#0F172A` to `#1E293B`).
- **Header:** Brand tier and model name in `headline-sm`.
- **Footer Grid:** Three quick-view metric columns (Cushion, Weight, Energy Return) separated by 1px vertical hairline dividers (`#334155`).
- **Interactive State:** Edge highlight transition to `#06B6D4` on press.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Size Selector Grid:** Multi-cell grid of shoe sizes (US / EU / CM toggle). Unselected cells have a transparent fill with a `#334155` border. Selected cell features a `#06B6D4` border and solid background with contrasting dark typography.
- **Search & Filter Inputs:** Deep container fill (`#1E293B`), inset 12px padding, inline leading search icon, clear button right-aligned. 

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Squared 18x18mm targets with 2px border `#334155`. When checked: `#06B6D4` fill with `#0F172A` checkmark.
- **Segmented Gait Filters (Road / Trail / Track):** Monolithic segmented bar with sliding indicator block in `#1E293B` and active text color shift.

### Sticky Dynamic Cart Bar (Mobile)
- Docked permanently to viewport bottom: height `72px`, background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.95)` with a 1px top border `#1E293B`.
- Left side: total price with dynamic tax calculation in `metric-val`.
- Right side: high-priority Add to Cart button spanning 55% of the bar width.
